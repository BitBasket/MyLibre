# Mission Statement

> A patient-owned, geographically chosen, interoperable personal CGM data layer: private by default, extensible by design, and independent of any vendor’s UI or regional lock.



What we're trying to do is the following:

  1. Self-sovereignty of data: Each user owns their own data by purchasing their own segmented cloud storage and cloud server, geographically local wherever possible, and
  under the GDPR in EU where not, and in an easy-to-deploy and maintain way: A DigitalOcean for-sale Droplet that they can just purchase adn click "Run" and pay the $2 a
  month or whatever. that they purchase via us so we can maintain it.
  2. Custom interfaces that aren't geo-locked and aren't platform-locked.
  3. Much more extensibililty with now-freed CGM per-minute information.
  4. Eventual support of all CGMs...
